# Excel analyst pack

`Fraud_Ops_Analysis.xlsx` is the recruiter- and stakeholder-facing handoff for this case study. It turns the committed aggregate evidence into a formula-driven workbook without distributing Kaggle raw data.

## Workbook map

- **Executive Summary** — decision, headline outcomes, evidence, risks, next gate, fallback, and claims boundary.
- **Policy Simulator** — yellow dropdown controls for operating posture and daily review capacity; KPI and action-routing outputs update through formulas.
- **Model Review** — locked out-of-time model ladder, promotion metrics, fallback, and uncertainty interpretation.
- **Weekly Monitoring** — calibration, drift, identity coverage, new-entity rate, alert precision, and pre-declared gates.
- **Data Profile** — population scale, rarity, identity coverage, concentration findings, implications, and controls.
- **Source Data** — aggregate policy, action, model, monitoring, and assumption tables used by workbook formulas.

## Claims boundary

The workbook is an offline decision aid. Prevented exposure and cost reduction are scenario outputs based on explicit assumptions; they are not realised production savings. Transaction amount is exposure, not confirmed loss.
